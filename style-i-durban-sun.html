Mind the gaps

Block out fabric stops the sun, but “light may filter in on the sides.” For a tighter fit, choose side channels to eliminate light gaps, or fit the blind outside the windowsill, extending 50mm over the edges. Fitted inside the sill, expect a 25mm gap on the control side and 10mm on the other.

How it works

From sunburn to sorted in four steps

  1. Measure

    Measure your own windows for an extra 10% discount. Or ask us to: the call-out fee is from R300 and is refunded when you order.

  2. Free quote
