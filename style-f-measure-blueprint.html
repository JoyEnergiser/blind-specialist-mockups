Sheet 02

The measuring rules

Read this for the 10% discount

If you are measuring to get the 10% self-measuring discount, this page is essential reading. All measurements are in millimetres.

R1

Choose how it mounts

Inside mount / top fix or outside mount / face fix. Inside mount is the most popular because your blinds are out of the way.

Inside mount · Outside mount

R2

Check the depth

To mount inside you need a depth of at least 60 mm inside your windowsill, from the window or burglar guards to the edge. Vertical blinds need 90 mm.

Tape against the sill edge

MIN.60mm

The Day/Night roller system with one bracket (two rollers on one bracket, inside the sill) needs 9 cm.

R3

Inside mount: measure 3×

Measure every window, as sizes may vary due to plastering. Take three measurements for width and height, and record the smallest. We subtract 10 mm for clearance. Guide example: width 1250 × height 980.

Three widths, three drops

Try it: record the smallest

Width
Height

R4

Outside mount: add the overlap

Measure the width and drop, adding at least 50 mm to each side, top and bottom. For vertical blinds, add 100 mm. Guide example: width 1350 × drop 1080.

Width and drop, over the opening

Try it: opening to outside size

Opening

Tiles or granite at the sill

If tiles or granite stick out at the side of the sill and would be in the way when the blind closes, you have two choices:

1. Measure the width up to the tiles. You will have a small opening at the sides of the blind.
2. Measure the tiles as a cutout in the blind. Cutouts are an extra cost.

Sliding doors

The guide’s diagram says it is best to use an outside mount for a sliding door and go 100 mm over the sides and top, to avoid door handles that stand out and get in the way of the blind.
