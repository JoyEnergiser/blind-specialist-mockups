Blind types explained

Zebra or Day/Night? A plain-English guide

Every blind on the Blind Specialist site, in one sentence, with a photo, what it suits and what to watch for.

Roller blind

Zebra

One blind, two layers

Translucent and opaque stripes sit in two layers. The front layer moves past the back one, so the stripes line up for an open or a closed effect.

Comes with a cassette.

vs
Roller system

Day/Night

Two blinds, one window

A Shade Weave roller keeps glare down by day, and a block-out roller gives you the dark and your privacy at night.

The 1-bracket version needs 9cm of depth in the sill.

Roller blind

Block-out roller blind

A roller blind in block-out fabric that stops sunlight.

Good for
Bedrooms and boardrooms.
Worth knowing
Light may filter in at the sides. Fit it outside the windowsill, 50mm over each edge, or ask about side channels.

Words you will hear

Block out
A fabric made to stop sunlight. It protects furniture from sun damage, but light can still creep in at the sides of a roller.
Light-filtering
A fabric that cuts glare but lets a little soft light glow through.
Shade Weave
A see-through sunscreen fabric. It reduces heat and glare and lets you keep your view.
Openness
How open the weave of a sunscreen fabric is. The Shade Weave fabrics listed are 5% open.
Inside mount (top fix)
The blind sits inside the window recess. You need at least 60mm of depth, and 90mm for vertical blinds.
Outside mount (face fix)
The blind sits on the wall or frame outside the opening, overlapping each side, the top and the bottom by at least 50mm (100mm for vertical).
Drop
The height of the blind. Measurements are given as width x drop, in millimetres.
Cassette
A neat cover over the rolled-up fabric. Optional on rollers, standard on Zebra blinds.
Headbar
A roller style that makes it easier to do your own fitting.
Pelmet or valance
A cover along the top of the blind that hides the headrail or pulley.
Chainless
A vertical blind with sealed bottom weights instead of a bottom chain: fewer repairs, safer around children and pets.
Motorisation
An optional extra on rollers. The one price on the site: Mastervue battery motor R2385, 1-channel remote control R343 (listed with Prem-Weave).
